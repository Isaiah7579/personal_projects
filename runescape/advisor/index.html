      Details
      Every item priced in range: picks, then items too dear for the cash left, then rejects grouped by reason. Red values are the ones that failed a check. Hover a column header for its formula. History columns are blank for items that never reached the price-history checks, and First traded is only checked for picks.
      Place each buy offer at the Buy at price: the instasell price plus 4.5% of the spread (instabuy − instasell), rounded up to the nearest 1k. Once bought, list at the current instabuy minus 10.6% of the spread, rounded down to the nearest 1k (Sell at is today's value, and what the lookup gives you); re-check and reprice every few hours. "Profit" is after 2% tax and assumes both offers fill at these prices. Uses only the free slots you pick and assumes your full buy limits are available. No item gets more than 31% of your cash (but always at least 1 unit); with fewer than 8 free slots that cap is scaled up by 8 ÷ free slots (1,024m with 6 slots is sized like 1,365m), so the stack still gets used, but offers never cost more than your cash. The rules are the strategy lab's evolved "soft rules" (filters and pricing tuned by evolution on past GE data, Bonds excluded); its simulated fills ignore other flippers, so start small. Items first traded in the last 30 days are flagged as new: their prices often slide or crash as supply grows. Skipped items stay skipped on this device until you clear them.
